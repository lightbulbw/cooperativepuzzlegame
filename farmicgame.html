Two friends(player 1 and player 2) explore a castle when suddenly the lights are blown out. To escape you have to help them solve puzzles and bring the moon phases to the knight at the entrance before their flashlight battery runs out.
The one and only flashlight you have is getting drained by the castle magic every time a player moves because it is technology from the future. So, be quick.
You can get 2 extra batteries by solving the Potion room puzzle, but will that be enough?

You can move around using arrow keys or w-a-s-d or h-j-k-l

There are 4 rooms and a corridor, each has one puzzle.
Puzzle descriptions:
Library:Find the code written in UV and enter it in the number lock in the chest(📦)
Potion room:Find a potion(🧪) and enter a color combination in the cauldron(⚗️)
Kitchen:Find a chest(📦) and solve a 2D Rubik's cube
The Mirror room: it has no entrance. If you dare enter you must move the mirrors(🪞) such that they look at each other.
Corridor:There are 2 pressure plates(🐾) that have to be simultaneously activated. However, their locations as well as traps(☠) are only marked in UV.

To restore health go to the Kitchen and collect food. However, there are only a total of 3 apples(🍎) so dont waist your hearts.

Can you help them get out on time?
O and be careful! There are rats(🐀).
